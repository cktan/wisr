# bench run 20261107-0403

Baseline of `bench/` (`run.sh`, `sqbench.sh`, `privcheck.sh`),
taken after the fixes of the aa review. `bench.old` was not run.

- Machine: Neoverse-V2, 4 cores, 15 GB, Linux 7.3.0-1004-gcp
- digres: 9807c8e5, `make -C dg release`; `digres --build` says:

      digres   -O2 -DNDEBUG
      sq       -O2 -DNDEBUG
      sm       -O3 -DNDEBUG
      RocksDB  9.11.2, static, -O3 -DNDEBUG

- PostgreSQL: postgres (PostgreSQL 18.6)
- RocksDB is 9.11.2 here, the version of the system's headers.
  The run of 20261101-0051 used 8.9.1: do not compare the two
  without that in mind.
- Servers: PostgreSQL with `shared_buffers = 2GB`, `jit = off`,
  `max_parallel_workers_per_gather = 0`, `autovacuum = off`;
  digres with `shared_buffers = 2GB`. Data under `bench/build/base`.

## privcheck.sh: what a privilege check costs a role

User instructions a statement, by `perf stat`, as the slope of two
run lengths; the median of 5 rounds. The server ran on CPU 2 and
pgbench on CPU 3 (`CLI_CPU=3`, as this machine has CPUs 0 to 3).
The gap is the role's count over the superuser's.

| sys | stmt | super | role | gap | gap % | spread % |
| --- | --- | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| dg | t | 23864 | 24207 | 344 | 1.4% | 0.05% |
| dg | fn | 24606 | 25064 | 458 | 1.9% | 0.04% |
| dg | fn16 | 47035 | 49087 | 2052 | 4.4% | 2.23% |
| dg | pt | 42325 | 42772 | 446 | 1.1% | 2.65% |
| pg | t | 22408 | 22710 | 302 | 1.3% | 92.26% |
| pg | fn | 23405 | 24402 | 997 | 4.3% | 15.05% |
| pg | fn16 | 55194 | 66553 | 11359 | 20.6% | 0.45% |
| pg | pt | 41823 | 42816 | 993 | 2.4% | 0.35% |

An EXECUTE check costs 114 instructions by `fn` and 107 by `fn16`
on digres, and 695 and 691 on PostgreSQL.

The spread of PostgreSQL's `t` (92%) and `fn` (15%) is too large
for those two gaps to mean anything.
